Frequently asked questions

Which standard and chemical composition apply to your GCr15 / 100Cr6 / SUJ2 bars?

GCr15 is supplied to GB/T 18254 with typical chemistry C 0.95–1.05%, Si 0.15–0.35%, Mn 0.25–0.45%, Cr 1.40–1.65%, P and S each ≤0.025%. Equivalents are EN ISO 683-17 100Cr6, JIS G4805 SUJ2 and ASTM A295 52100. The exact heat analysis is stated on the MTC shipped with each bundle.

What hardness can I expect from spheroidize-annealed GCr15?

Spheroidize-annealed (+A) bars are supplied for improved machinability, typically ≤ 207 HBW; the certified value for your heat number is stated on the MTC. As-rolled, peeled and ground conditions are also available.

Do you also supply GCr15SiMn, 100CrMn6 or SUJ3?

Yes. Alongside GCr15 we supply GCr15SiMn and its common equivalents 100CrMn6 / SUJ3 for larger cross-sections, in the same diameter range and delivery conditions. Tell us your national standard and we will confirm the closest available grade.

Which international grades are equivalent to GCr15?

GCr15 is commonly specified alongside 100Cr6, SUJ2 and AISI 52100; GCr15SiMn corresponds to 100CrMn6 / SUJ3. Send your national standard and we will confirm the closest available grade and its MTC chemistry.

What bar diameters can you supply?

Round bars from 20 to 200 mm in diameter. Other sizes can be checked against mill availability on request.

Can bars be cut to length before shipment?

Yes. Our in-house band saw cuts to your required length with clean cut faces and accurate lengths, ready for forging or machining.

What delivery conditions are available?

As-rolled, spheroidize-annealed, peeled or ground. Spheroidize-annealed stock is commonly chosen where machinability matters.

Is an MTC provided for each heat?

Yes. Every heat number carries its own Mill Test Certificate, and SGS / BV / TÜV pre-shipment inspection is welcome.

Can I combine bearing steel bars with steel coil in one order?

Yes. Many customers consolidate bearing steel bars with galvanized, cold rolled or galvalume coil in a single shipment from Shanghai. Ask for a consolidated FOB / CIF quotation.

What is the MOQ?

Our standard MOQ is 20 MT per specification. Mixed sizes can be combined in one container. Send your grade, dimensions and destination port for a confirmed FOB Shanghai / CIF / CFR quotation within 24 hours.

How fast can you deliver?

The fastest lead time is 15 days from order confirmation for stocked specifications. Mill orders take longer depending on grade and coating weight; we confirm the exact delivery schedule in writing with every quotation.
