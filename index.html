ABOUT US

Your Reliable Steel Partner in Shanghai

Shanghai Hongfu Industrial Co., Ltd. is a steel trading and processing company headquartered in Shanghai, China — the country’s largest hub for steel commerce and export logistics. We supply galvanized steel coil (GI), cold rolled coil (CRC), pickled steel coil and high-carbon chromium bearing steel, backed by long-term cooperation with leading Chinese mills.

Beyond supply, we deliver value-added processing — coil slitting and cut-to-length for flat products, and precision saw cutting for bearing steel bars — so our customers receive material that is ready for production. With import & export qualification and convenient access to the ports of Shanghai, we serve distributors, manufacturers and fabricators worldwide.

Our philosophy is simple: honest quality, transparent pricing, and service that makes sourcing from China easy.

Quality First

Every batch is traceable to the producing mill and shipped with a Mill Test Certificate. Third-party inspection (SGS / BV / TÜV) is welcome.

One-Stop Processing

Slitting, cut-to-length and bar cutting under one roof — material arrives at your factory ready for the next step, saving you time and cost.

Fast & Transparent

Quotations within 24 hours, clear Incoterms pricing (FOB / CIF / CFR), and dedicated follow-up from inquiry to delivery.
