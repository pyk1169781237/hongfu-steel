ABOUT US

Your Reliable Steel Partner in Shanghai

Shanghai Hongfu Industrial Co., Ltd. is a steel trading and processing company headquartered in Shanghai, China — the country’s largest hub for steel commerce and export logistics. FAQ FOR BUYERS

Frequently Asked Questions

MOQ, certificates, Incoterms, processing and quality — answered plainly

Why is GCr15 bearing steel your flagship product?

Bearing steel is our core product line. We supply GCr15, GCr15SiMn and the equivalents 100Cr6, SUJ2, AISI 52100 and 100CrMn6 / SUJ3 from leading Chinese special steel mills, cut Ø 20–200 mm bars to length in-house, and issue an MTC for every heat number. Standard MOQ 20 MT, fastest lead time 15 days, FOB Shanghai / CIF / CFR.

Which standard and chemical composition apply to your GCr15 round bar?

GCr15 is specified to GB/T 18254 with typical chemistry C 0.95–1.05%, Si 0.15–0.35%, Mn 0.25–0.45%, Cr 1.40–1.65%, P and S each ≤0.025%. Equivalent designations include EN ISO 683-17 100Cr6, JIS G4805 SUJ2 and ASTM A295 52100. Exact values are confirmed on the MTC of each heat number.

What is the MOQ for galvanized steel coil?

Our standard MOQ is 20 MT per specification. Mixed sizes can be combined in one container. Send your grade, dimensions and destination port for a confirmed FOB Shanghai / CIF / CFR quotation within 24 hours.

Do you provide a Mill Test Certificate with every shipment?

Yes. Every batch ships with a Mill Test Certificate (MTC) and full heat-number traceability to the producing mill. Third-party inspection by SGS, BV or TÜV is welcome before shipment.

Which Incoterms and markets do you serve?

We quote FOB Shanghai, CIF and CFR. From the Port of Shanghai we ship to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, Latin America and beyond, with a complete document set: MTC, packing list, commercial invoice, certificate of origin and shipping documents.

How quickly can I get a steel coil quotation?

Within 24 hours. Send the grade, size, coating weight, quantity and destination port by e-mail or WhatsApp and we will return a clear Incoterms-based offer.

Can you slit master coils and cut sheets to length?

Yes. Three in-house lines handle coil slitting (0.3–3.0 mm thick, up to 200 m/min, slit width from 20 mm), cut-to-length levelling and shearing, and bearing steel bar saw cutting (diameter 20–200 mm). Material arrives ready for your production line.

What grades and coating weights are available for GI coil?

Standard supply covers DX51D+Z and S250GD–S550GD with zinc coating Z60–Z275 g/m². Other grades, dimensions and coating weights are available on request.

Is GCr15 / 100Cr6 / SUJ2 bearing steel available cut to length?

Yes. We supply GCr15, GCr15SiMn, 100Cr6, SUJ2, AISI 52100 and 100CrMn6 / SUJ3 round bars in as-rolled, spheroidize-annealed, peeled or ground condition, saw-cut to your required length with an MTC for every heat number.

How is steel coil packed for sea freight?

Seaworthy export packing: waterproof wrap, steel strapping, wooden cradles and VCI anti-rust protection, engineered for long-distance ocean transport. Sheets are stacked and banded on wooden pallets.

What are your payment terms?

We issue a proforma invoice after order confirmation; a 30% deposit starts production and the balance is settled against documents before shipment. Terms can be discussed for long-term partners.

How do you handle quality claims?

Every batch is traceable by heat number and covered by an MTC. Verified quality issues are eligible for replacement, and we welcome pre-shipment third-party inspection.

How fast can you deliver?

The fastest lead time is 15 days from order confirmation for stocked specifications. Mill orders take longer depending on grade and coating weight; we confirm the exact delivery schedule in writing with every quotation.
