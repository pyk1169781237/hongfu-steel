55% Al-Zn coated coil for corrosive and high-temperature environments

Galvalume coil is specified where standard galvanized coating reaches its limit: coastal and industrial atmospheres, roofing exposed to heat cycling, oven and appliance components, and agricultural or utility buildings. The aluminum-rich coating forms a durable barrier, while zinc provides sacrificial protection at cut edges and scratches.

Our galvalume steel coil supply covers AZ70 to AZ200 g/m² coating weights to ASTM A792, and we can slit master coils into narrow strips from 20 mm wide or level them into cut-to-length sheets. Prepainted (PPGI) coils in RAL colours with PE, SMP or HDP top coats are also available when a finished colour surface is required. Send the coating weight, grade, dimensions and destination port — we quote within 24 hours with clear Incoterms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between galvalume and galvanized coil?

Galvanized coil is coated with essentially pure zinc, while galvalume uses a 55% aluminum-zinc alloy. The aluminum adds barrier and heat resistance, while zinc still protects cut edges sacrificially. Galvalume generally performs better in corrosive or high-temperature environments.

Which coating weights are available?

We supply AZ70 to AZ200 g/m² to ASTM A792. Tell us the service environment and we will recommend a coating weight.

Can galvalume coil be slit to narrow strips?

Yes. Slit widths start at 20 mm with clean edges and stable width tolerance, produced on our in-house 0.3–3.0 mm slitting line.

Do you supply prepainted PPGI coil as well?
