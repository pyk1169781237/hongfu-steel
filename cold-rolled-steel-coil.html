Cold rolled coil for precision stamping, appliances and automotive components

Cold rolled steel coil is produced by reducing hot rolled coil at room temperature, giving a smooth, clean surface and tight dimensional tolerance. That makes CRC the preferred substrate for appliance panels, electrical cabinets, automotive components, furniture, tubes and precision stamping, where surface finish and formability directly affect yield.

We supply master coil as well as value-added forms: cut-to-length cold rolled sheets levelled and banded for direct feeding into punching or laser lines, small cut-to-size automotive stamping blanks stacked and wrapped for press feeding, and slit strips from 20 mm wide for tube mills and cold-forming lines. Electro-galvanized sheet is also available in SECC / SECCN5 / SECD / SECE, DC01+ZE and DC04+ZE where a spangle-free zinc surface is required. In-house processing, quality & export

Slitting, cut-to-length and bar cutting

Shanghai Hongfu Industrial Co., Ltd. runs three in-house lines so material arrives ready for your production line: a cold-rolled slitting line for material 0.3–3.0 mm thick at speeds up to 200 m/min with slit widths from 20 mm and clean edges; a levelling and cut-to-length line producing flat sheets and blanks to your required length; and a band saw for bearing steel round bars from 20 to 200 mm in diameter. Processing covers CRC, electro-galvanized, hot-dip galvanized, galvalume, pickled, stainless and aluminium coil.
